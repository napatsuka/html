Eight seats at a single plank of hinoki. Each piece shaped with red-vinegar rice and the day's fish.

Ginza 7-chome, Tokyo
Omakase only, two seatings nightly at 17:30 and 20:30

Fish rested.
Vinegar aged two years.

The fish we buy each morning at Toyosu is never served the same day. We rest it for one to ten days, watching the texture and fat, and serve it when the flavour peaks.

Our rice is seasoned with two red vinegars made from sake lees aged for two years. More salt than sweetness, served at body temperature, firm enough to hold and soft enough to fall apart in your mouth.

We keep eight seats so that each piece can be made at the pace you eat.

Reiji Sawai, owner and chef

Frequently asked questions

Can you accommodate allergies?

Tell us when you book and we'll replace what we can. We can leave out all crustaceans and shellfish. Unfortunately we can't serve guests who don't eat raw fish.

Do you speak English?

The chef and staff speak basic English, and we have an English menu at the counter.

What if I arrive late?

Each seating starts together, so late guests join from the course in progress. If you're running late, please call us.

Can I book on behalf of a hotel guest?

Yes. Hotel concierges can use this form. Please put the guest's name in the name field and your own phone number for contact.

Is there a dress code? Can I take photos?

There's no dress code. Photos of your food are welcome without flash, as long as other guests aren't in frame.
